I am an evolutionary ecologist who studies the drivers of establishment and spread of invasive populations. My current research focuses on understanding and predicting the invasion of the Spotted Lanternfly (*Lycorma delicatula*) in the United States, using a combination of field studies and mathematical models. Check out my research projects [here](research.md) for more details. I am currently working as a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Environmental Studies department at [Macalester College](https://www.macalester.edu/environmental-studies/) after teaching at [Bryn Mawr College](https://www.brynmawr.edu/inside/academic-information/departments-programs/biology) for two academic years. I teach a variety of courses, including _Ecology and the Environment_, _Invasion Biology_, _Biodiversity Conservation_, _Ethics in Biology_ and _Animal Behavior_. See a short description of my courses [here](teaching.md).
